# Role-led tower artwork

Every tower family now has original artwork that shows its weapon or purpose.
All 17 families have separate silhouettes and three visibly different tier frames;
none are recolored aliases of another family.

The atlas and matching portraits are generated by:

```sh
node scripts/generate-tower-art.mjs
```

- Atlas: `src/assets/towers/role-towers.svg` — 2304 × 1536 pixels, 51 cropped frames.
- Measurements: `src/assets/towers/role-towers.json` — frame crop, ground origin, and weapon outlet.
- Review sheet: `src/assets/towers/tower-contact-sheet.svg` — all families and tiers.
- Provenance and SHA-256 checksums: `src/assets/towers/provenance.json`.
- Art dedication: `src/assets/towers/LICENSE.txt` — CC0-1.0 for these new art assets only.

These are original SVG drawings made with code. They contain no downloaded images,
third-party textures, traced assets, or image-generation outputs. Existing asset
packs remain in the repository, but their tower frames are no longer selected by
the game. Maps, enemies, walking animation, and audio keep their previous assets
and licenses.

## Visual vocabulary

| Family | Visible identity and tier growth |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow | Stone watchtower with a real brass crossbow, additional bows, and blue pennants; tier II has reveal lanterns. |
| Cannon | Slanted iron cannon barrel on wheels, expanding to a battery. |
| Mortar | Upright bronze bombard tubes in a low fortified siege emplacement. |
| Ice | A field of angular ice crystals; tier II adds the Aurora revealing lens. |
| Earth | Stacked natural boulders, roots, extra standing stones, and a rune capstone. |
| Mage | A tall violet-roofed wizard spire with side turrets, orbit ring, and hanging crystals. |
| Tesla | A copper-wound electrical coil, conducting side arms, and lightning terminals. |
| Poison | Brass chemical vats, green liquids, valves, and a pressure outlet. |
| Prism | A large bright optical lens held in a bronze fork, with satellite lenses. |
| Trap | A low road plate with raised steel jaws, spikes, gears, and upgraded net wires. |
| Sonic | A flared brass horn array, adding differently aimed horns at higher tiers. |
| Barracks | A broad blue-roofed garrison with an open gate, shields, flanking towers, and flags. |
| Void | Two dark obelisks framing an oval vortex; higher tiers add binding rings and unstable cracks. |
| Solar | A fire-filled bronze brazier inside a sun halo with stronger rays. |
| Chrono | A glass hourglass and moving-clock visual vocabulary, with exposed clockwork gears. |
| Truesight | A large eye-shaped observatory, iris lens, and an expanding targeting halo. |
| Nexus | A concentric orbital mechanism with an elemental core and multiple intersecting rings. |

`src/asset-art.ts` uses the same source frames for the tower library, selected-tower
portrait, and placed defense. `AssetViews` places each frame by its measured ground
anchor, and muzzle effects/projectiles start at the measured weapon outlet. The
artwork rasterizes once on load instead of redrawing SVG geometry every frame.

Firing effects match the roles: arrows, straight cannonballs, high-arc mortar shells,
ice shards, rocks, mage orbs, Tesla lightning, poison globules, straight prism beams,
trap jaws, sonic rings, melee slashes, void implosions, fireballs, temporal clocks,
revealing beams, and multi-element Nexus orbs. Existing build/upgrade/hit clocks and
reduced-motion behavior remain in place.
