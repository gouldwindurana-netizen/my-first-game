# Mossguard expansion roadmap

The pasted feature ideas are a roadmap. The user selected **24-fps animation and core controls** for this update. The later items below are planned work, not current game capabilities.

## This update

- Articulated walking for every active moving unit: goblin, runner, brute, warlord.
- Eight generated key poses per unit, expanded to 24 frames with motion-compensated interpolation and a 24-fps presentation clock.
- Stable body scale, pause, reduced motion, frost-slow cadence, and a close-up walking preview.
- First, Last, Strongest, and Fastest targeting priorities.
- Paid tower relocation preserving level, priority, and cooldown.
- 1×, 2×, and 3× speed, plus upgrade/sell hotkeys.
- Existing five battlefields, seven tower types, foundation capacity, Starfall, early-call gold, local bests, balance simulations, and automated browser tests remain available.

## Combat expansion

- Flying-only targeting when flying enemy units are introduced.
- Elemental reactions: ice/fire armor shatter, Tesla/poison chains, earth/ice frozen ground.
- Nexus auras, Prism beam splitting, and adjacency bonuses.
- Mana or gem-powered freeze-all, reinforcements, repair, and additional spells.
- Movable commander with three abilities and stage leveling.
- Configurable supply caps and 6–8 tower loadouts from an expanded 17-tower roster.

## Enemy expansion

- Healers, shielders, tower-disabling saboteurs, and counter-play against troops/traps.
- Swift, regenerating, armored, and explosive elite affixes.
- Stealth units and Truesight detection.
- Dragon, ice dragon, and necromancer boss phases with attack telegraphs.
- Optional risk/reward wave modifiers.

## Biomes and stage events

- Extend the current five maps toward the twelve concept-map campaign.
- Frost Peaks ice shortcuts; Black Forest fog and blocked sightlines.
- Desert storms, burrowing units, lava vents, and fire immunity.
- Lush Valley healing springs; mire slowing for enemies and troops.
- Crystal-node tower boosts; Ancient City wall constraints.
- Sky Island bridge controls and aerial waves; Wasteland scrap and siege.
- Dragon’s Lair boss rush.
- Route-changing bridges, rockslides, and gates.

## Progression and economy

- Between-wave relic choices, endless mode, seeded daily challenges, and challenge rules.
- Tower mastery, unlockable skins, prestige, codex entries, and achievements.
- Interest on saved gold, duplicate build-price scaling, and cosmetic/loadout/revive gem sinks.

## Interface, audiovisual, and technical work

- Per-wave tower damage/kills report, weakness previews, route arrows, build undo, and fuller touch range previews.
- Volume, particle and performance settings, colorblind-safe palettes, and further accessibility options.
- Biome weather and lighting events, ambience/music, dynamic music layers, boss shake, and optional elemental damage numbers.
- Versioned save export/import and installable offline PWA support.
- Extend the existing headless balance and browser smoke suites for each added system.

Each stage should add the necessary art, enemy counters, UI, and reproducible tests together; unused controls and unsupported mechanics should not be presented as playable features.
