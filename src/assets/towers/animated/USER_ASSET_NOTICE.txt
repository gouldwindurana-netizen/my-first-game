Runtime sheets use 192x192 cells in lossless WebP at their authored 30 FPS cadence.

below 2048 pixels. Small portraits derive from the supplied icons. The normalized
ground anchor remains (0.5, 0.8984375). Input hashes, output hashes, dimensions,
and conversion commands are recorded in normalization.json.

There is one idle and one attack clip for each of the 17 matching tower IDs.
Masonry stays stationary while composited light, particle, and attack effects
animate. These are not individually repainted mechanical or character animations,
and no optical-flow interpolation is claimed. The pack has no distinct tier or
specialization drawings. Gameplay tiers retain size, level pips, branch labels,
and upgrade effects while using the supplied painted design.
